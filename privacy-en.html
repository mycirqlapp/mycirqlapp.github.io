Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

MyCIRQL (the “App”) is designed with your privacy as the highest priority. The App has no servers run by the developer: it does not send the information you register to the developer's servers, and the developer cannot obtain it. This policy explains how the App handles information, including the cases in which information leaves your device.

1. Information We Handle

The App handles the following information, which you enter and register yourself:

  • Names of people, shops, and places; readings (pronunciation guides); and relationships, including the lines in the family tree and relationship chart
  • Contact details such as phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, postal codes, and workplaces
  • Dates such as birthdays, anniversaries, and dates of passing
  • Photos (including images of business cards), text read from business cards, notes, and categories

All of this is information you register within the App. The App never collects information without your knowledge. App settings such as display options, the lock, and notification times are also stored on your device.

2. Where Information Is Stored

The information you register is stored on your device. In addition, on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, if you are signed in to iCloud with your Apple account, the information you register (including photos and business-card images) is automatically synced to your own private area in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). There is no setting within the App to turn this sync on or off. When iCloud is unavailable, the information is stored only on your device. On Android, depending on your device settings, it is saved in your own Google account backup (photos are not included in this backup and are carried over only in a direct device-to-device transfer). The developer cannot view or obtain this information. How content stored in iCloud or your Google account is handled follows your own settings and your agreements with Apple and Google.

3. Sending Information Externally

The App does not send the information you register to the developer's servers, and it makes no connections for advertising or analytics. The App communicates outside your device only in the following cases:

  • iCloud sync (iPhone, iPad, Mac): As described in Section 2, the App communicates with Apple's iCloud (your own private area).
  • Auto-filling an address from a postal code (only when the display language is Japanese): Once you have entered all 7 digits of a postal code, the App automatically sends that postal code to an external address-lookup service (zipcloud) after a short delay; no button press is needed. Only the postal code is sent; your name and other personal information are not sent. The service receives the information that normally accompanies any internet connection (such as your IP address). When the display language is not Japanese, this communication does not take place.
  • Checking in-app purchases: The App communicates with the Apple App Store or Google Play to purchase, restore, and confirm your purchase status (Section 8).
  • Handing information to other apps and services when you tap: See Section 5.

4. Use of Device Features (Permissions)

With your permission, the App may use the following device features. They are used only for purposes within the App, and nothing obtained is sent to the developer.

  • Camera: To take a photo on the spot and add it to a person or place, and to scan business cards and similar items (document scan).
  • Photos: To choose photos on your device and add them to an album or a business card (the App receives only the photos you select in the photo picker), and, only when you choose to, to save images such as the relationship chart to the Photos app. You can also add images you choose in the Files app and similar places.
  • Contacts: To import contacts from your device for registration. This is read-only; the App never changes the contacts on your device. The items read are names, phone numbers, email addresses, messaging and social-media IDs, and company names.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / Passcode (on Android: biometric authentication / device PIN or pattern): To unlock the app when you enable the launch lock (identity verification).
  • Notifications: To show reminders such as birthdays, anniversaries, and dates of passing. These notifications are scheduled and displayed on your device; the App does not use notifications sent from a server (push notifications).

Text recognition (OCR) of business cards and the like is performed entirely on your device; images and recognized text are never sent externally.

5. Integration with Standard Apps, Sharing, and Exporting

The App has no built-in feature to send email or make calls on its own. Only when you tap “Call,” “Email,” “Open in Maps,” and so on does your device's standard Phone, Mail, Messages, or map app open, and that contact's phone number, email address, or address is handed to that app. When you choose “Open in Maps,” the address is passed to the map service you select (Apple Maps or Google Maps). When you choose “WhatsApp,” a WhatsApp link (wa.me) containing the phone number is opened, in the WhatsApp app or, on a device without it, in your web browser. When you choose the Gmail or Outlook app, the recipient address is passed to that app. Any actions after that are handled by those apps and services and by you.

Sharing and exporting: Sharing contacts (for example by AirDrop), exporting CSV or PDF files or images of the relationship chart or family tree, and the full backup work only when you ask for them. The App creates a file on your device and opens your device's standard share screen (or a screen for choosing where to save the file). You choose where it goes. The App does not send these files to the developer's servers or anywhere else on its own. A shared file contains the information of the contacts you selected (depending on your choices, this can include photos, business-card images, relationships, and notes), and a full backup contains everything you have registered. These files are not encrypted, so the App cannot control how they are handled after you send or save them. A shared file you receive is registered in the App only if you choose the items to import and save them.

6. Provision to Third Parties

The App never provides or sells your information to any third party, except for what is described in Sections 2, 3, 5, and 8 (iCloud sync, postal-code lookup, sharing and handing information to other apps that you yourself initiate, and in-app purchases). It does not serve advertising or track your usage, and it contains no third-party software (SDK) for analytics, advertising, or crash collection.

7. Deleting Data: Types of Data Deleted and Retention Period

You can delete the information you register in MyCIRQL yourself at any time, as follows.

  • Delete individually: Choose “Delete” on the detail screen of a contact or album in the App, and it is deleted immediately. If you sync with iCloud, the deletion is also reflected on your other devices that use the same Apple account.
  • Delete everything: If you uninstall (delete) the App, all of the App's data stored on your device is deleted.

The data that can be deleted consists of all the information listed in Section 1 of this policy, “Information We Handle” (names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, postal codes, dates, photos, business-card images, notes, categories, and so on). Because this information is never stored on the developer's servers, the developer has no deletion to carry out; deletion is complete the moment you perform the steps above. No data remains with the developer. Files you created by sharing or exporting, and data held by the people you shared it with, cannot be deleted from within the App.

To delete copies synced to iCloud (iPhone, iPad, Mac) or saved in your Google account backup (Android), use Apple's iCloud settings or the Google One settings of your Google account, respectively. Uninstalling the App alone does not delete the copies on the iCloud or Google side. These are your own personal areas managed by Apple or Google, which the developer cannot access.

8. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time “Premium” purchase. Payment is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and the App and its developer never obtain or store payment details such as credit card numbers. The only thing the App keeps is whether you have purchased Premium, which is stored on your device. On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, it is not part of the iCloud sync, and the App confirms it from your App Store purchase record; on Android, depending on your device settings, it is also included in your Google account backup.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be changed as needed. Any change takes effect when it is posted on this page.
